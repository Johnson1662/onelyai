# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Creator growth and research operators working on a desktop or laptop. They review a public-evidence candidate pool, prioritize creators, inspect supporting facts, and record human decisions before outreach.

## Product Purpose

Onely Creator Outreach is an evidence-backed acquisition workspace for a private beta. It turns a candidate CSV into a reviewable pipeline with explainable scores, public evidence, controlled pre-outreach statuses, funnel visibility, and an auditable human override path. Success means an operator can move from imported candidates to a confident next action without losing context.

## Brand Personality

简约、克制、清晰。 The interface should feel calm and trustworthy while keeping the next decision obvious.

## Anti-references

No specific anti-reference was provided. Keep the product task-first and avoid decorative treatment that competes with evidence review.

## Design Principles

- Make evidence and next actions easy to scan.
- Keep scoring explainable at the point of decision.
- Use restrained visual emphasis for priority and status.
- Preserve context while moving through the pipeline.
- Make human changes visible and auditable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No additional requirements were provided. Preserve standard keyboard access, visible focus states, readable contrast, semantic controls, and reduced-motion support.
